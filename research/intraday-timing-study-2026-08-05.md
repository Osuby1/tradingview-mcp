# Intraday timing study (Omar hypothesis, 2026-08-05)

Claim tested: open fades -> lunch drifts lower -> strength into close.
Data: 59 trading days of 30-min bars (SPY/QQQ/IWM, ~Jun-Aug 2026, ET times).

**VERDICT: REFUTED - the current pattern is roughly the INVERSE.**
- First 30 min: flat-to-up (SPY +0.004%, up 61% of days); only QQQ leans lower.
- LUNCH 11:30-13:30 = the STRONGEST segment on all three (QQQ +0.13%, 63% up).
- Afternoon 13:30-15:00 = the fade (QQQ up only 34% of days); avg path peaks
  13:30-14:30 ET and bleeds into the close.
Likely source of the perception: up-GAPS often fade (gap vs cash-session
conflation).

Caveats: effects are 3-13 bps, single 60-day melt-up regime, mostly within
noise; lunch-strength + afternoon-fade are the two cross-ETF-consistent bits.
Tiebreaker only - never overrides plan levels.

**Adopted guidance:** BUY window ~10:45-11:30 ET (spreads healed + ahead of
the strong segment; options especially - measured open spreads 16-20% healing
to <8% within the hour). SELL/bank window ~13:30-14:30 ET (the peak).
Applied immediately: GE 8/6 pre-decided exit to be worked 12:30-13:30 CT.
Re-test quarterly - intraday seasonality is regime-dependent.
