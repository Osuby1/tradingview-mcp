The 7/20 grade was partly apples-to-oranges: `og_shadow.py` has always computed
Heikin-Ashi internally, but the chart it was graded against was still on candles
until the 7/23 pipeline flip. This run is the first like-for-like comparison.

**The Magical proxy is effectively solved.** CCI(20) on HA typical price now
correlates 0.991 with the chart's Magical oscillator. That was the biggest
unknown in the port and it is no longer a source of error.

**The remaining error is Chandelier flip TIMING, not rules.** Of the 70 mode
mismatches, 54 (77%) sit within 5 bars of the shadow's own flip, and the median
is 1 bar. The two engines agree on direction and disagree on the exact bar they
turn. The skew is one-directional - 48 of 70 are shadow=sell / chart=long -
so the shadow flips OUT of long earlier than the chart does, consistent with a
seeding or data-vintage difference in the ATR(1) ratchet (yfinance daily bars vs
the chart's own feed), not a different rule.

**Not promotable yet.** 85.8% is well short of what it would take to trust this
lane to replace chart reads, and the errors are concentrated exactly where it
matters most - fresh flips, which are the only signals the scan acts on. Next
step is to diagnose the one-bar ATR seeding offset; if that closes, the mode
match should jump, because everything else already agrees.

## Why this had not been run since 7/20

`build_universe_results.py` changed the results schema (`mode`/`fresh` became
`ce_mode`, with freshness moving into `hits`). `og_shadow.py` read only the old
field names, so from that day it exited immediately with "nothing to grade
against". Nothing in `run_eod_chain.bat` calls it, so the failure was silent.
Fixed 7/25 with a `normalize_truth()` shim that reads both schemas.
