## Bottom line first: the automation failed and this run was rescued by hand

The scheduled EOD chain **failed at 15:25** and produced nothing. The cause was
the headless-Claude sweep running out of session budget partway through the
490-name loop - the same failure mode as 7/21.

The safety gate worked exactly as designed. `verify_eod_output.py` saw no scan
output, refused to compile a workbook, exited non-zero, and wrote
*"do not act on any workbook dated 2026-07-24"* into the log. **No stale report
was ever shipped.** That is the 7/21 lesson holding.

The run was then redone manually and committed at **23:01** - about eight hours
late. Everything below comes from that manual recovery.

The permanent fix went in on 7/25: a plain Node process (`og_sweep_cdp.mjs`)
drives the sweep over CDP instead of a headless LLM turn, so it cannot run out of
budget. It has since been soak-tested on the full 494-name universe.
